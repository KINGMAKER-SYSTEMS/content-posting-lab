OpenAI quietly turns on its agent runtime for every account

By R. Okafor & J. Lindqvist — 06:12 ET

The company flipped the switch without a keynote, a blog post, or so much as a changelog entry. Internal dashboards seen by The Terminal Wire show the staged rollout that began Friday night accelerating through the weekend.

By morning the rollout had reached 100% of accounts — without a single public announcement.

Developers discovered the change when long-running agent jobs stopped returning quota errors. Pricing pages still describe the runtime as "research preview," and three teams told us their existing orchestration stacks broke silently overnight.
